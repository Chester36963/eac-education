## Q01. APGP / 等差等比數列

**分類：** log-products
**主要技巧：** seq-product-sum

### 中文題目
設 \(A(n)\) 為等差數列第 \(n\) 項，\(A(3)=-14,A(10)=7\)。
(a) 求 \(A(1)\)。（2 分）
(b) 假設 \(G(n)=3^{A(n)}\)，求最大的 \(k\)，使 \(G(1)G(2)\cdots G(k)<100000\)。（5 分）

### English Version
Let \(A(n)\) be the nth term of an AP, with \(A(3)=-14,A(10)=7\).
(a) Find \(A(1)\).
(b) Suppose \(G(n)=3^{A(n)}\). Find the greatest \(k\) such that \(G(1)G(2)\cdots G(k)<100000\).

## Q03. APGP / 等差等比數列

**分類：** roots-sequences
**主要技巧：** seq-vieta

### 中文題目
設 \(p\) 及 \(q\) 分別為二次方程 \((x+2)(x-2)=8(x-1)\) 的兩根之和與積。
(a) 寫出 \(p\) 及 \(q\) 的值。
(b) 某等比數列的首項及第二項分別為 \(\log q\) 及 \(\log p\)。求 \(\alpha\) 的最大值，使該數列第 \((\alpha+1)\) 項與第 \((2\alpha+1)\) 項之和小於 \(\log 2^{2020}\)。

### English Version
Let \(p\) and \(q\) be respectively the sum and product of the roots of \((x+2)(x-2)=8(x-1)\).
(a) Write down the values of \(p\) and \(q\).
(b) A geometric sequence has first term \(\log q\) and second term \(\log p\). Find the greatest value of \(\alpha\) such that the sum of its \((\alpha+1)\)th and \((2\alpha+1)\)th terms is less than \(\log 2^{2020}\).

## Q04. APGP / 等差等比數列

## Q08. APGP / 等差等比數列

**分類：** sequence-applications
**主要技巧：** seq-interleave

### 中文題目
設 \(a_n\) 為數列 \(X\) 的第 \(n\) 項。對所有正整數 \(n\)，\(a_{2n+2}=a_{2n}+2\) 及 \(a_{2n+1}=a_{2n-1}-3\)。已知 \(a_2=7\)、\(a_3=63\)。
(a) 求 \(a_1\) 及 \(a_4\)。
(b) 設 \(m\) 為整數。求最小的 \(m\)，使數列 \(X\) 首 \((2m-1)\) 項之和大於 \(2251\)。
(c) 數列 \(Y\) 的第 \(n\) 項為 \(b_n=1.2^{a_{2n}}\)，其中 \(n\) 為正整數。求最大的 \(k\)，使數列 \(Y\) 首 \(k\) 項之積小於 \(10^{30}\)。

### English Version
Let \(a_n\) be the \(n\)th term of sequence \(X\). For every positive integer \(n\), \(a_{2n+2}=a_{2n}+2\) and \(a_{2n+1}=a_{2n-1}-3\). It is given that \(a_2=7\) and \(a_3=63\).
(a) Find \(a_1\) and \(a_4\).
(b) If \(m\) is an integer, find the smallest value of \(m\) such that the sum of the first \((2m-1)\) terms of \(X\) is greater than \(2251\).
(c) Let \(b_n\) be the \(n\)th term of sequence \(Y\), where \(b_n=1.2^{a_{2n}}\) for every positive integer \(n\). Find the greatest value of \(k\) such that the product of its first \(k\) terms is less than \(10^{30}\).
