### Q1
If \(a>0\), simplify the expression. 若 \(a>0\)，化簡下式。
\[\frac{\sqrt[4]{a^{2/3}}\cdot a^{-9/8}}{(a^{1/2})^{-4}}.\]
A. \(1/a^{71/24}\)　B. \(a^{3/32}\)　C. \(a^{25/24}\)　D. \(a^{79/24}\)

The fourth root contributes exponent 1/6. Subtract the denominator exponent −2. 四次根式嘅指數為 1/6，除以分母時須減去 −2。\(1/6-9/8+2=25/24\)。**C**

### Q2
For rational \(n\), evaluate the expression. 設 \(n\) 為有理數，求下式的值。
\[\frac{14^{2n+1}}{2^{2n+3}7^{2n+1}-7^{2n+2}2^{2n}}.\]
A. \(1/2730\)　B. \(1/14\)　C. \(2\)　D. \(2744\)

Divide numerator and denominator by \(2^{2n}7^{2n+1}\). 分子分母同除以此正公因式，得 \(2/(8-7)=2\)。**C**

### Q3
Given \(6^p=x,\ 3^{2p}=y\) for rational \(p\), express \(324^p\) in terms of \(x,y\). 設 \(p\) 為有理數，已知上述兩式，以 \(x,y\) 表示 \(324^p\)。
A. \(2xy\)　B. \(x^2y/3\)　C. \(x^2\sqrt y\)　D. \(x^2y\)

Since \(324=6^2\cdot 3^2\), raising to \(p\) gives \(x^2y\)。將等式兩邊取 \(p\) 次冪再代入已知量。**D**

### Q4
Find \(x\). 求 \(x\)。
\[(1/2)^x(1/4)^{2x/3}(1/8)^{5x/6}=2^{-1015}.\]
A. −630　B. −210　C. 210　D. 630

Equating exponents in base 2 gives \(-x-4x/3-5x/2=-1015\). 化為底數 2 後比較指數，得 \(-29x/6=-1015\)，故 \(x=210\)。**C**

### Q5
![同題原圖重繪](figures/q5.svg)

The figure shows \(y=n^x-m\), where \(m,n\) are constants. The y-intercept is −1. Which statement must be true? 圖中函數的縱截距為 −1，其中兩個參數均為常數。下列何者必定正確？
A. \(m=1,0<n<1\)　B. \(m=1,n>1\)　C. \(m=2,0<n<1\)　D. \(m=2,n>1\)

At zero, \(1-m=-1\) gives \(m=2\). The graph decreases, so \(0<n<1\)。代入零求得平移量，再由遞減性判斷底數範圍。**C**

### Q6
![同題原圖重繪](figures/q6.svg)

The figure shows \(y=-a^x,-b^x,-c^x\). All three bases are positive and different from 1. Which statement must be true? 圖中三條曲線的底數均為正數且不等於 1。下列何者必定正確？
A. \(a>b>c\)　B. \(c>a>b\)　C. \(c>b>a\)　D. \(b>a>c\)

At \(x=1\), the ordinates from bottom to top are \(-c,-a,-b\). Comparing the ordinates and reversing signs gives \(c>a>b\)。在橫坐標 1 比較三條曲線，將所得負值同乘 −1，不等號須反向。**B**

### Q7
![同題原圖重繪](figures/q7.svg)

The figure shows \(y=a^x, a>1\). Find \(1/a^{4/5}\) correct to one decimal place. 圖示 \(y=a^x\) 的圖像，其中 \(a>1\)。求 \(1/a^{4/5}\)，答案準確至小數點後一位。
A. 0.2　B. 0.3　C. 3.0　D. 5.3

Read the ordinate at \(x=-0.8\), because \(1/a^{4/5}=a^{-0.8}\). 由橫坐標 −0.8 讀取縱坐標，約為 0.3。**B**

### Q8
Given \(3^y81^x/27^{2y}=1\) with \(x,y>0\), find \(x:y\). 已知上式且兩數均為正，求其比。
A. 1:1　B. 1:4　C. 4:5　D. 5:4

The exponent in base 3 is zero. 寫成底數 3 得 \(y+4x-6y=0\)，所以 \(4x=5y\)，\(x:y=5:4\)。**D**

### Q9
For \(a,b>0\), simplify and express using positive indices. 設兩變量均為正，化簡下式並以正指數表示。
\[\sqrt{(a^2b^{-3})^5}\div\frac{(2ab^{-1})^3}{ab}\times4ab^2.\]
The radical simplifies to \(a^5b^{-15/2}\) and the divisor to \(8a^2b^{-4}\). 將根式與除式分別化簡，再乘以除式的倒數，得 \((4/8)a^{5-2+1}b^{-15/2+4+2}=a^4/(2b^{3/2})\)。

### Q10
Solve 解方程 \(4^x+2^{2x+1}+2^{2x-1}=7/8\)。
Factor out \(4^x\). 提取公因式得 \((1+2+1/2)4^x=7/8\)，故 \(4^x=1/4\)，\(x=-1\)。

### Q11
Solve the simultaneous equations. 解聯立方程。
\[2^{2x-3y}4^{3x-4}=8^{1-x}32^{6y}=128.\]
Equate each exponent to 7 in base 2. 逐式化為底數 2 得 \(8x-3y=15\) 和 \(-3x+30y=4\)。Multiply the first equation by 10 and add the second. 第一式乘 10 再加第二式，得 \(77x=154\)，故 \(x=2,y=1/3\)。

### Q12
Solve 解方程 \(2x^{-2/3}+13x^{-1/3}-7=0\)。
Set \(u=1/\sqrt[3]{x}\), where \(x\ne0\). 代換後得 \((2u-1)(u+7)=0\)。Thus \(u=1/2,-7\) gives \(x=8,-1/343\) under real cube-root interpretation. 在實數立方根定義下兩根均成立；若課程將一般分數指數限定於正底數，則只有 8。原卷只列 8，沒有寫明定義域。**待覆核課程定義及呈現方式。**

### Q13
The city has 365400 vehicles this year. The number increases by 5% each year. (a) Find the number after 7 years to 3 significant figures. (b) Will the number after 14 years be twice this year's number? Explain. (c) Winnie says the percentage increase over the first 7 years is half that over the first 14 years. Do you agree? Explain.
某市今年有 365400 輛汽車，每年增加 5%。(a) 求 7 年後的數目，取三位有效數字。(b) 14 年後會否是今年的兩倍？解釋答案。(c) Winnie 認為首 7 年的百分增幅是首 14 年的一半。你同意嗎？解釋答案。

(a) Multiply by seven annual growth factors. 七年後共 \(365400(1.05)^7\approx514000\) 輛（三位有效數字）。(b) No. 否，\(1.05^{14}\approx1.97993<2\)。(c) Disagree. 不同意。Let \(g=1.05^7-1>0\). The 14-year increase as a fraction of the initial count is \((1+g)^2-1=2g+g^2>2g\)。後七年以較高的數目為基數，所以十四年的增幅大於首七年增幅的兩倍。

### Q14
An antique was worth $200000 at the beginning of 2000. After \(t\) years its value in dollars is \(W=ka^t\), where \(a,k\) are constants. It was worth $600000 at the beginning of 2010. (a) Find \(k\). (b) Wilson says it will be worth nine times its initial value at the beginning of 2020. Do you agree? Explain.
一件古董在 2000 年初價值 200000 元，\(t\) 年後的價值（元）為 \(W=ka^t\)，其中兩個參數均為常數。2010 年初價值為 600000 元。(a) 求 \(k\)。(b) Wilson 認為 2020 年初的價值是 2000 年初的九倍。你同意嗎？解釋答案。

(a) At \(t=0\), \(k=200000\)。以起始價值求得常數。(b) Yes. 同意。The ten-year multiplier is \(a^{10}=600000/200000=3\). Two such intervals give \(a^{20}=9\)。經過兩個十年期，價值是原來的九倍。
