### 中文題目

設 \[\begin{gathered}f(x) =3x^3 +ax^2 \\ -(2b-11a)x -35\end{gathered}\]，其中 \(a,b\) 為常數。\(f(x)\) 被 \(3x^2-bx-1\) 除的餘式為 \(-32\)。
(a) 求 \(a,b\)。
(b) 求 \(f(x)\) 被 \(3x+5\) 除的餘式。
(c) Edwin 聲稱 \(f(x)=0\) 的所有根均為有理數。你同意嗎？解釋。

### English Question

Let \[\begin{gathered}f(x) =3x^3 +ax^2 \\ -(2b-11a)x -35\end{gathered}\], where \(a,b\) are constants. When \(f(x)\) is divided by \(3x^2-bx-1\), the remainder is \(-32\).
(a) Find \(a,b\).
(b) Find the remainder when \(f(x)\) is divided by \(3x+5\).
(c) Edwin claims that all the roots of \(f(x)=0\) are rational. Do you agree? Explain.

### 中文詳解

(a) 設商為 \(cx+d\)。比較首項及常數項得 \(c=1,d=3\)。再得 \(a=9-b\) 及 \(2b-11a=1+3b\)，解得 \(a=-1,b=10\)。
(b) 此時 \[\begin{gathered}f(x) =3x^3 -x^2 \\ -31x -35 \\ =(3x+5)(x^2-2x-7)\end{gathered}\]。故餘式為 \(f(-\frac53)=0\)。
(c) 根為 \(-\frac53\) 及 \(1\pm2\sqrt2\)，後兩根是無理數，故不同意。

### English Solution

(a) Let the quotient be \(cx+d\). Comparing leading and constant coefficients gives \(c=1,d=3\). Then \(a=9-b\) and \(2b-11a=1+3b\). Solving gives \(a=-1,b=10\).
(b) Now \[\begin{gathered}f(x) =3x^3 -x^2 \\ -31x -35 \\ =(3x+5)(x^2-2x-7)\end{gathered}\]. Hence the remainder is \(f(-\frac53)=0\).
(c) The roots are \(-\frac53\) and \(1\pm2\sqrt2\). The latter two are irrational, so Edwin is incorrect.

### 中文題目

設 \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(2x-3)(3x^2+ax+b)\end{gathered}\]，其中 \(a,b\) 為常數。\(f(x)\) 被 \(x-2\) 除的餘式為 \(5a+2\)，被 \(x+1\) 除的餘式為 \(-35\)。
(a) 求 \(a,b\)。
(b) 有人聲稱 \(f(x)=6x-9\) 至少有一個無理根。你同意嗎？解釋。

### English Question

Let \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(2x-3)(3x^2+ax+b)\end{gathered}\], where \(a,b\) are constants. When \(f(x)\) is divided by \(x-2\), the remainder is \(5a+2\). When divided by \(x+1\), the remainder is \(-35\).
(a) Find \(a,b\).
(b) Someone claims that \(f(x)=6x-9\) has at least one irrational root. Do you agree? Explain.

### 中文詳解

(a) 由 \(f(2)=5a+2\) 得 \(3a-b=10\)；由 \(f(-1)=-35\) 得 \(-a+b=4\)，故 \(a=7,b=11\)。
(b) 把 \(6x-9=3(2x-3)\) 移到左邊，分解得 \[\begin{gathered}(2x-3)(3x^2+7x+8) \\ =0\end{gathered}\]。二次式判別式為 \(49-96=-47<0\)，唯一實根是有理數 \(\frac32\)，沒有無理根，故不同意。

### English Solution

(a) \(f(2)=5a+2\) gives \(3a-b=10\); \(f(-1)=-35\) gives \(-a+b=4\). Hence \(a=7,b=11\).
(b) Move \(6x-9=3(2x-3)\) to the left and factor: \[\begin{gathered}(2x-3)(3x^2+7x+8) \\ =0\end{gathered}\]. The quadratic discriminant is \(49-96=-47<0\), so the only real root is the rational number \(\frac32\). There is no irrational root; disagree.

### 中文題目

設 \(f(x)\) 為三次多項式，被 \(x+1\) 及 \(x-2\) 除的餘式分別為 \(-5\) 及 \(148\)，並可被 \(5x^2+7x+3\) 整除。
(a) 求它被 \(5x^2+7x+3\) 除的商。
(b) Peter 聲稱 \(f(x)=0\) 有 3 個相異實根。你同意嗎？解釋。

### English Question

Let \(f(x)\) be a cubic polynomial. Its remainders on division by \(x+1\) and \(x-2\) are \(-5\) and \(148\) respectively. It is divisible by \(5x^2+7x+3\).
(a) Find the quotient when divided by \(5x^2+7x+3\).
(b) Peter claims that \(f(x)=0\) has 3 distinct real roots. Do you agree? Explain.

### 中文詳解

(a) 設 \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(ax+b)(5x^2+7x+3)\end{gathered}\]。代入 \(-1\) 得 \(-a+b=-5\)，代入 2 得 \(2a+b=4\)。故 \(a=3,b=-2\)，商為 \(3x-2\)。
(b) 二次式的判別式為 \[\begin{gathered}7^2 -4(5)(3) = \\ -11<0\end{gathered}\]，只有一次因式給出實根 \(x=\frac23\)。因此恰有一個實根，故不同意。

### English Solution

(a) Write \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(ax+b)(5x^2+7x+3)\end{gathered}\]. Substituting \(-1\) gives \(-a+b=-5\); substituting 2 gives \(2a+b=4\). Thus \(a=3,b=-2\) and the quotient is \(3x-2\).
(b) The quadratic discriminant is \[\begin{gathered}7^2 -4(5)(3) = \\ -11<0\end{gathered}\]. Only the linear factor gives a real root, \(x=\frac23\). There is exactly one real root, so disagree.

### 中文題目

多項式 \(f(x)\) 滿足 \(f(-2)=0\)。它被 \(x^2+x-1\) 除的商為 \(4x^2+x+4\)，餘式為 \(kx+k-5\)。
(a) 求 \(k\)。
(b) 求 \(f(-\frac14)\)。
(c) 把 \(f(x)\) 完全因式分解。

### English Question

A polynomial \(f(x)\) satisfies \(f(-2)=0\). When \(f(x)\) is divided by \(x^2+x-1\), the quotient is \(4x^2+x+4\) and the remainder is \(kx+k-5\).
(a) Find \(k\).
(b) Find \(f(-\frac14)\).
(c) Factorise \(f(x)\) completely.

### 中文詳解

(a) 由除法恆等式 \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(x^2+x-1)(4x^2+x+4) \\ +kx +k -5\end{gathered}\]，代入 \(-2\) 得 \(0=18-k-5\)，故 \(k=13\)。
(b) 把 \(-\frac14\) 代入該恆等式，得 \(f(-\frac14)=0\)。
(c) 兩個已知根給出因式 \(x+2\) 及 \(4x+1\)。把展開式 \[\begin{gathered}4x^4 +5x^3 +x^2 \\ +16x +4\end{gathered}\] 除以兩因式得 \(x^2-x+2\)。故 \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(x+2)(4x+1)\\ {}\cdot(x^2-x+2)\end{gathered}\]。最後的二次式判別式為 \(-7\)，不能再分解成實一次因式。

### English Solution

(a) Use \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(x^2+x-1)(4x^2+x+4) \\ +kx +k -5\end{gathered}\]. At \(x=-2\), \(0=18-k-5\), giving \(k=13\).
(b) Substitute \(-\frac14\) into this identity to get \(f(-\frac14)=0\).
(c) The two known roots give factors \(x+2\) and \(4x+1\). Dividing the expanded polynomial \[\begin{gathered}4x^4 +5x^3 +x^2 \\ +16x +4\end{gathered}\] by these factors gives \(x^2-x+2\). Hence \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(x+2)(4x+1)\\ {}\cdot(x^2-x+2)\end{gathered}\]. The last factor has discriminant \(-7\), so has no real linear factors.

(b) Substituting \(x=-\frac23\) gives zero, so \(3x+2\) is a factor.

## Q7

Source: S4/more about polynomials/POLYNOMIALS REVIEWS 6.pdf · page 1 · Q7

Category: polynomial-equations / poly-root-nature

Difficulty: hard

Disposition: publish → import-polynomial-d7e1e22278a4ea38

### 中文題目

設 \[\begin{gathered}g(x) =x^3 +3x^2 \\ +(r-4)x -r\end{gathered}\]，其中 \(r\) 為整數。
(a) \(x-1\) 是否 \(g(x)\) 的因式？解釋。
(b) 把 \(g(x)\) 因式分解。
(c) 學生聲稱恰有兩個 \(r\) 值令 \(g(x)=0\) 恰有兩個相異實根。你同意嗎？解釋。

### English Question

Let \[\begin{gathered}g(x) =x^3 +3x^2 \\ +(r-4)x -r\end{gathered}\], where \(r\) is an integer.
(a) Is \(x-1\) a factor of \(g(x)\)? Explain.
(b) Factorise \(g(x)\).
(c) A student claims that exactly two values of \(r\) make \(g(x)=0\) have exactly two distinct real roots. Do you agree? Explain.

### 中文詳解

(a) \[\begin{gathered}g(1) =1 +3 +r -4 -r \\ =0\end{gathered}\]，所以是因式。
(b) 相除得 \[\begin{gathered}g(x) \\ =(x-1)(x^2+4x+r)\end{gathered}\]。
(c) 恰有兩個相異實根有且只有兩種可能：二次式有異於 1 的重根，則 \(16-4r=0\)，得 \(r=4\)，根為 \(1,-2\)；或二次式有兩個相異實根且其中一根是 1，則 \(1+4+r=0\)，得 \(r=-5\)，此時 \(g(x)=(x-1)^2(x+5)\)。
若 \(r>4\)，只有實根 1；若 \(r<4\) 且 \(r\ne-5\)，有三個相異實根。因此恰有 \(r=4,-5\) 兩值可行，故同意。

### English Solution

(a) \[\begin{gathered}g(1) =1 +3 +r -4 -r \\ =0\end{gathered}\], so yes.
(b) Division gives \[\begin{gathered}g(x) \\ =(x-1)(x^2+4x+r)\end{gathered}\].
(c) Two possibilities exhaust exactly two distinct real roots. First the quadratic has a repeated root other than 1: \(16-4r=0\), giving \(r=4\) and roots \(1,-2\). Second it has two distinct roots, one equal to 1: \(1+4+r=0\), giving \(r=-5\) and \(g(x)=(x-1)^2(x+5)\).
For \(r>4\) only the root 1 is real; for \(r<4\) other than \(-5\) there are three distinct real roots. Thus precisely \(r=4,-5\) work; agree.
