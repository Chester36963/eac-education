### 中文題目

設 \(f(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx-14\)，其中 \(a,b\) 為常數。當 \(f(x)\) 被 \(x+1\) 除時，餘式為 \(-36\)。已知 \(f(x)\) 可被 \(x-2\) 整除。

(a) 求 \(a,b\)。

(b) 有學生聲稱方程 \(f(x)=0\) 的所有根都是實數。你是否同意？解釋你的答案。

### English Question

Let \(f(x)=x^{3}+ax^{2}+bx-14\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are constants. When \(f(x)\) is divided by \(x+1\), the remainder is \(-36\). It is given that \(f(x)\) is divisible by \(x-2\).

(a) Find \(a\) and \(b\).

(b) A student claims that all the roots of the equation \(f(x)=0\) are real. Do you agree? Explain your answer.

### 中文詳解

第 (a) 部分

餘式條件給出 \(f(-1)=-36\)：

\[
-1+a-b-14=-36,\qquad a-b=-21. \tag{1}
\]

因式條件給出 \(f(2)=0\)：

\[
8+4a+2b-14=0,\qquad 2a+b=3. \tag{2}
\]

聯立 \(1\)、\(2\)，得

\[
a=-6,\qquad b=15.
\]

第 (b) 部分

\[
f(x)=x^3-6x^2+15x-14.
\]

因為 \(x-2\) 是因式，

\[
f(x)=(x-2)(x^2-4x+7).
\]

剩餘二次式的判別式為

\[
\Delta=(-4)^2-4(1)(7)=-12<0.
\]

它沒有實根，所以不同意該聲稱。

### English Solution

Part (a)

Since the remainder when divided by \(x+1\) is \(-36\),

\[
f(-1)=-36.
\]

\[
-1+a-b-14=-36
\]

\[
a-b=-21. \tag{1}
\]

Since \(f(x)\) is divisible by \(x-2\),

\[
f(2)=0.
\]

\[
8+4a+2b-14=0
\]

\[
2a+b=3. \tag{2}
\]

Solving \(1\) and \(2\),

\[
a=-6,\qquad b=15.
\]

Part (b)

Then

\[
f(x)=x^3-6x^2+15x-14.
\]

Since \(x-2\) is a factor,

\[
f(x)=(x-2)(x^2-4x+7).
\]

For \(x^2-4x+7=0\),

\[
\Delta=(-4)^2-4(1)(7)=16-28=-12<0.
\]

The quadratic factor has no real roots. Therefore not all roots of \(f(x)=0\) are real. The claim is disagreed.

### 中文題目

已知 \(P(x)=x^2-x-6\) 及 \[\begin{gathered}Q(x) =x^3 -4x^2 -3x \\ +18\end{gathered}\]。
(a) 把 \(P(x)\) 及 \(Q(x)\) 因式分解。
(b) 求兩式的最大公因式及最小公倍式。

### English Question

Given \(P(x)=x^2-x-6\) and \[\begin{gathered}Q(x) =x^3 -4x^2 -3x \\ +18\end{gathered}\].
(a) Factorise \(P(x)\) and \(Q(x)\).
(b) Find their G.C.D. and L.C.M.

### 中文詳解

(a) \(P(x)=(x-3)(x+2)\)。由 \(Q(-2)=0\)，除以 \(x+2\) 得 \(x^2-6x+9\)，故 \(Q(x)=(x+2)(x-3)^2\)。
(b) 公因式取最低次冪，公倍式取最高次冪：最大公因式 \((x-3)(x+2)\)；最小公倍式 \((x+2)(x-3)^2\)。

### English Solution

(a) \(P(x)=(x-3)(x+2)\). As \(Q(-2)=0\), division by \(x+2\) gives \(x^2-6x+9\), so \(Q(x)=(x+2)(x-3)^2\).
(b) Take minimum factor powers for the G.C.D. and maximum powers for the L.C.M.: G.C.D. \((x-3)(x+2)\); L.C.M. \((x+2)(x-3)^2\).

### 中文題目

(a) 寫出 \(2x^3-x^2-6x-4\) 被 \(2x^2-3x-9\) 除的商及餘式。
(b) 設 \[\begin{gathered}g(x) \\ =(2x^3-x^2-6x-4) \\ -(ax+b)\end{gathered}\]，其中 \(a,b\) 為常數。已知 \(g(x)\) 可被 \(2x^2-3x-9\) 整除。
(i) 寫出 \(a,b\)。
(ii) 把 \(g(x)\) 因式分解。
(iii) 設 \(f(x)=x^3-7x-6\)，化簡 \[\begin{gathered}\frac1{f(x)} \\ -\frac1{g(x)}\end{gathered}\]。

### English Question

(a) Write down the quotient and remainder when \(2x^3-x^2-6x-4\) is divided by \(2x^2-3x-9\).
(b) Let \[\begin{gathered}g(x) \\ =(2x^3-x^2-6x-4) \\ -(ax+b)\end{gathered}\], where \(a,b\) are constants. It is given that \(g(x)\) is divisible by \(2x^2-3x-9\).
(i) Write down \(a,b\).
(ii) Factorise \(g(x)\).
(iii) Let \(f(x)=x^3-7x-6\). Simplify \[\begin{gathered}\frac1{f(x)} \\ -\frac1{g(x)}\end{gathered}\].

### 中文詳解

(a) 用長除法得商 \(x+1\)，餘式 \(6x+5\)。
(b)(i) 須減去整個餘式，故 \(a=6,b=5\)。
(ii) \[\begin{gathered}g(x) \\ =(2x^2-3x-9)(x+1) \\ =(2x+3)(x-3)(x+1)\end{gathered}\]。
(iii) \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(x+1)(x+2)(x-3)\end{gathered}\]。通分後分子為 \((2x+3)-(x+2)=x+1\)，約去後得 \(\frac1{(x-3)(x+2)(2x+3)}\)。
原式須滿足 \[\begin{gathered}x\ne -2, -\frac32, \\ -1,3\end{gathered}\]；約去因式不能恢復 \(x=-1\)。

### English Solution

(a) Long division gives quotient \(x+1\) and remainder \(6x+5\).
(b)(i) Subtract the entire remainder: \(a=6,b=5\).
(ii) \[\begin{gathered}g(x) \\ =(2x^2-3x-9)(x+1) \\ =(2x+3)(x-3)(x+1)\end{gathered}\].
(iii) \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(x+1)(x+2)(x-3)\end{gathered}\]. Taking a common denominator leaves numerator \((2x+3)-(x+2)=x+1\). Cancel this factor to obtain \(\frac1{(x-3)(x+2)(2x+3)}\).
The original expression requires \[\begin{gathered}x\ne -2, -\frac32, \\ -1,3\end{gathered}\]; cancellation does not restore \(x=-1\).

### 中文題目

設 \(f(x)=2x^3-x^2+ax+b\)，其中 \(a,b\) 為實數。已知 \(f(x)\) 可被 \(x+2\) 整除，而 \(f(x)\) 被 \(x+1\) 除時餘式為 \(10\)。

(a) 求 \(a,b\)。

(b) Susan 聲稱：對任何整數 \(n\)，\(f(n)f(n+1)\) 必為偶數。你是否同意？解釋你的答案。

### English Question

Let \(f(x)=2x^3-x^2+ax+b\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are real constants. It is given that \(f(x)\) is divisible by \(x+2\). When \(f(x)\) is divided by \(x+1\), the remainder is \(10\).

(a) Find the values of \(a\) and \(b\).

(b) Susan claims that \(f(n)f(n+1)\) is even for any integer \(n\). Do you agree? Explain your answer.

### 中文詳解

第 (a) 部分

\(x+2\) 是因式，所以

\[
f(-2)=0.
\]

\[
-16-4-2a+b=0
\Rightarrow b=20+2a. \tag{1}
\]

又因除以 \(x+1\) 的餘式為 \(10\)，

\[
f(-1)=10.
\]

\[
-2-1-a+b=10
\Rightarrow b-a=13. \tag{2}
\]

聯立 \(1\)、\(2\)，得

\[
a=-7,\qquad b=6.
\]

第 (b) 部分

因此

\[
f(x)=2x^3-x^2-7x+6.
\]

不需要逐個代入整數。

對任何整數 \(n\)，模 \(2\) 下有

\[
f(n)\equiv-n^2-n\equiv n^2+n=n(n+1)\equiv0\pmod2.
\]

因為相鄰整數 \(n,n+1\) 其中一個必為偶數，故 \(f(n)\) 已經是偶數；所以 \(f(n)f(n+1)\) 必為偶數。

Susan 的聲稱正確。

### English Solution

Part (a)

Since \(x+2\) is a factor,

\[
f(-2)=0.
\]

\[
-16-4-2a+b=0
\]

\[
b=20+2a. \tag{1}
\]

Since the remainder when divided by \(x+1\) is \(10\),

\[
f(-1)=10.
\]

\[
-2-1-a+b=10
\]

\[
b-a=13. \tag{2}
\]

Solving \(1\) and \(2\),

\[
a=-7,\qquad b=6.
\]

Part (b)

\[
f(x)=2x^3-x^2-7x+6.
\]

For any integer \(n\), consider \(f(n)\) modulo 2:

\[
f(n)\equiv -n^2-n \equiv n^2+n=n(n+1)\pmod 2.
\]

Since \(n(n+1)\) is always even,

\[
f(n)
\]

is even for every integer \(n\). Hence \(f(n)f(n+1)\) is even. Susan's claim is agreed.

### 中文題目

設 \(f(x)=3x^3+8x^2+ax+b\)，其中 \(a,b\) 為常數。當 \(f(x)\) 被 \(x^2-3x+10\) 除時，餘式為 \(-12x-160\)。

(a) 求 \(a,b\)。

(b) \(3x-1\) 是否 \(f(x)\) 的因式？解釋你的答案。

(c) 有人聲稱方程 \(f(x)=0\) 的所有根都是正數。你是否同意？解釋你的答案。

### English Question

Let \(f(x)=3x^3+8x^2+ax+b\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are constants. When \(f(x)\) is divided by \(x^2-3x+10\), the remainder is \(-12x-160\).

(a) Find \(a\) and \(b\).

(b) Is \(3x-1\) a factor of \(f(x)\)? Explain your answer.

(c) Someone claims that all the roots of \(f(x)=0\) are positive. Do you agree? Explain your answer.

### 中文詳解

第 (a) 部分

三次被二次式除，商式必是一次式；又因 \(f(x)\) 的首項是 \(3x^3\)，先設

\[
Q(x)=3x+d.
\]

寫出除法恆等式：

\[
f(x)=(x^2-3x+10)(3x+d)-12x-160.
\]

展開右方：

\[
f(x)=3x^3+(d-9)x^2+(18-3d)x+(10d-160).
\]

比較 \(x^2\) 係數，\(d-9=8\)，所以 \(d=17\)。

因此

\[
a=18-3(17)=-33,\qquad b=10(17)-160=10.
\]

第 (b) 部分

因式 \(3x-1\) 的根是 \(x=\frac{1}{3}\)。

\[
f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{9}+\frac{8}{9}-11+10=0.
\]

所以 \(3x-1\) 是 \(f(x)\) 的因式。

第 (c) 部分

代入 \(a=-33,b=10\) 並完成因式分解：

\[
f(x)=3x^3+8x^2-33x+10=(3x-1)(x+5)(x-2).
\]

根為 \(\frac{1}{3},-5,2\)。

其中兩個是負數，所以不同意該聲稱。

### English Solution

Part (a)

Let the quotient be \(3x+d\). Then

\[
f(x)=(x^2-3x+10)(3x+d)-12x-160.
\]

Expanding,

\[
f(x)=3x^3+(d-9)x^2+(30-3d-12)x+(10d-160).
\]

Compare the \(x^2\) coefficient:

\[
d-9=8
\]

\[
d=17.
\]

Thus

\[
a=18-3(17)=-33,\qquad b=10(17)-160=10.
\]

Part (b)

\[
f(x)=3x^3+8x^2-33x+10.
\]

Check \(x=\frac{1}{3}\):

\[
f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{9}+\frac{8}{9}-11+10=0.
\]

Therefore \(3x-1\) is a factor of \(f(x)\).

Part (c)

Factorizing,

\[
f(x)=(3x-1)(x+5)(x-2).
\]

The roots are

\[
\frac{1}{3},\ -5,\ 2.
\]

Since \(-5\) is negative, not all roots are positive. The claim is disagreed.

### English Question

(a) Let the quotient be \(Ax+B\) when \(f(x)\) is divided by \(x^2+3x+5\), where \(A\) and \(B\) are constants. Find \(A\) and \(B\). Hence, find \(f(0)\).

(b) Factorize \(f(x)\) completely.

### 中文詳解

第 (a) 部分

三次式除二次式，商式必為一次式。

因此

\[
f(x)=(x^2+3x+5)(Ax+B)-26x+170.
\]

\[
f(2)=28.
\]

\[
15(2A+B)-52+170=28
\Rightarrow2A+B=-6. \tag{1}
\]

\[
f(-5)=0.
\]

\[
15(-5A+B)+130+170=0
\Rightarrow-5A+B=-20. \tag{2}
\]

\[
A=2,\qquad B=-10.
\]

因此

\[
f(0)=5(-10)+170=120.
\]

第 (b) 部分

\[
f(x)=(x^2+3x+5)(2x-10)-26x+170.
\]

展開：

\[
f(x)=2x^3-4x^2-46x+120.
\]

完全因式分解為

\[
f(x)=2(x+5)(x-3)(x-4).
\]

### English Solution

\[
f(x)=(x^2+3x+5)(Ax+B)-26x+170.
\]

\[
f(2)=28.
\]

\[
15(2A+B)-52+170=28
\]

\[
2A+B=-6. \tag{1}

\[
f(-5)=0.
\]

\[
15(-5A+B)+130+170=0
\]

\[
-5A+B=-20. \tag{2}
\]

\[
A=2,\qquad B=-10.

Then

\[
f(0)=5(-10)+170=120.

\[
f(x)=(x^2+3x+5)(2x-10)-26x+170.
\]

Expanding,

\[
f(x)=2x^3-4x^2-46x+120.

### 中文題目

設 \(f(x)=4x^3-7x-3\)。
(a) 證明 \(2x+1\) 是 \(f(x)\) 的因式。
(b) 已知 \[\begin{gathered}g(x) \\ =f(x)\div(15-10x)\end{gathered}\]。
(i) 以 \(x\) 表示 \(g(x)\)。
(ii) 從而求 \(1-g(x)\) 的最小值。

### English Question

Let \(f(x)=4x^3-7x-3\).
(a) Show that \(2x+1\) is a factor of \(f(x)\).
(b) It is given that \[\begin{gathered}g(x) \\ =f(x)\div(15-10x)\end{gathered}\].
(i) Express \(g(x)\) in terms of \(x\).
(ii) Hence find the minimum value of \(1-g(x)\).

### 中文詳解

(a) \[\begin{gathered}f(-\frac12) = \\ -\frac12 +\frac72 \\ -3 =0\end{gathered}\]，由因式定理得證。
(b)(i) 相除及分解得 \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(2x+1)(2x-3)(x+1)\end{gathered}\]。由 \(15-10x=-5(2x-3)\)，得 \[\begin{gathered}g(x) = \\ -\frac15(2x+1)(x+1)\end{gathered}\]，其中 \(x\ne\frac32\)。

### English Solution

(a) \[\begin{gathered}f(-\frac12) = \\ -\frac12 +\frac72 \\ -3 =0\end{gathered}\], so the factor theorem applies.
(b)(i) Division and factorisation give \[\begin{gathered}f(x) \\ =(2x+1)(2x-3)(x+1)\end{gathered}\]. Since \(15-10x=-5(2x-3)\), \[\begin{gathered}g(x) = \\ -\frac15(2x+1)(x+1)\end{gathered}\], with \(x\ne\frac32\).
(ii) Complete the square: \[\begin{gathered}1 -g(x) \\ =\frac25(x+\frac34)^2 \\ +\frac{39}{40}\end{gathered}\]. Its minimum is \(\frac{39}{40}\), attained at \(x=-\frac34\), which is allowed.

### English Question

Let \(f(x)=2x^3+kx^2+5x+6\), where \(k\) is a constant. It is given that when \(f(x)\) is divided by \(ax^2-4x-1\), the quotient is \(x-2\) and the remainder is \(bx+c\), where \(a,b,c\) are constants.

(b) It is given that \(g(x)=f(x)-x^2+2x\). Factorize \(g(x)\).

由除法恆等式，

\[
2x^3+kx^2+5x+6=(ax^2-4x-1)(x-2)+bx+c.
\]

\[
(ax^2-4x-1)(x-2)=ax^3+(-2a-4)x^2+7x+2.

\[
a=2,\qquad 7+b=5,\qquad 2+c=6.

\[
a=2,\qquad b=-2,\qquad c=4.

\[
g(x)=(x-2)(x-3)(2x+1).
\]

\[
2x^3+kx^2+5x+6=(ax^2-4x-1)(x-2)+bx+c.
\]

\[
(ax^2-4x-1)(x-2)=ax^3+(-2a-4)x^2+7x+2.
\]

Compare coefficients:

\[
a=2,\qquad 7+b=5,\qquad 2+c=6.
\]

Hence

\[
a=2,  b=-2,  c=4.
\]

Also

\[
k=-2a-4=-8.
\]

Part (b)

\[
f(x)=2x^3-8x^2+5x+6.
\]

Therefore

\[
g(x)=f(x)-x^2+2x=2x^3-9x^2+7x+6.
\]

Factorizing,

\[
g(x)=(x-2)(x-3)(2x+1).
\]

\[D(x)=3x^2+ax+2,\]
\[\begin{gathered}f(x)=D(x)(2x+b)\\ {}+cx-6.\end{gathered}\]
