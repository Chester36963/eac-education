### English Question

The H.C.F. and L.C.M. of three polynomials are \(4x^2-4x+1\) and \[\begin{gathered}36(2x-1)^3 \\ \cdot(2x+1)^4 \\ \cdot(2x+3)^2\end{gathered}\] respectively. Two of them are \(4(2x-1)^2(2x+1)^3\) and \(6(2x-1)^2(2x+3)\). Find the third.

### 中文詳解

最大公因式為 \((2x-1)^2\)。第三式的正係數與 4、6 的最小公倍數須為 36、最大公因數須為 1，因此為 9。三個括號因式的最高指數 3、4、2 均須由第三式提供，故為 \[\begin{gathered}9(2x-1)^3 \\ \cdot(2x+1)^4 \\ \cdot(2x+3)^2\end{gathered}\]。代回三式核對可得原定的最大公因式及最小公倍式。

兩個二次多項式的最大公因式為 \(3x-2\)，最小公倍式為 \(6x^3+5x^2-3x-2\)。

### English Question

Two degree-2 polynomials have H.C.F. \(3x-2\) and L.C.M. \(6x^3+5x^2-3x-2\).
(a) Factorise the L.C.M.
(b) Find the two polynomials.

### 中文詳解

(a) \[\begin{gathered}6x^3 +5x^2 -3x -2 \\ =(3x-2)(2x+1)(x+1)\end{gathered}\]。
(b) 每個二次式包含共同的一次因式及餘下其中一個因式。取標準本原代表，兩式為 \[\begin{gathered}(3x-2)(2x+1) =6x^2 \\ -x -2\end{gathered}\] 及 \[\begin{gathered}(3x-2)(x+1) =3x^2 \\ +x -2\end{gathered}\]，次序可互換。若公因式／公倍式只定義至非零常數倍，則相應常數倍視為等價。

### English Solution

(a) \[\begin{gathered}6x^3 +5x^2 -3x -2 \\ =(3x-2)(2x+1)(x+1)\end{gathered}\].
(b) Each quadratic contains the common linear factor and one of the other factors. The standard primitive representatives are \[\begin{gathered}(3x-2)(2x+1) =6x^2 \\ -x -2\end{gathered}\] and \[\begin{gathered}(3x-2)(x+1) =3x^2 \\ +x -2\end{gathered}\] (in either order). (c) Find their H.C.F. and L.C.M.

### English Solution

(c) H.C.F. \((2x-3)(x-5)\); L.C.M. \[\begin{gathered}(2x-3) \cdot(x-5) \\ \cdot(2x+1) \\ \cdot(3x+2)\end{gathered}\].
