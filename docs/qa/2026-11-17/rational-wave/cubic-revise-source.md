### 中文題目

設 \[\begin{gathered}f(x)=6x^3+kx^2\\{}-(k-2)x-6\end{gathered}\]，其中 \(k\) 為常數。已知 \(f(x)=(x-2)(ax^2+bx+c)\)，其中 \(a,b,c\) 為常數。

(a) 求 \(a,b,c\)。

(b) 設 \(g(x)=3x^2-7x+2\)。有人聲稱 \(f(x)=g(x)\) 有3個相異實根。你是否同意？解釋。

### English Question

Let \[\begin{gathered}f(x)=6x^3+kx^2\\{}-(k-2)x-6\end{gathered}\] where \(k\) is a constant. It is given that \(f(x)=(x-2)(ax^2+bx+c)\), where \(a,b,c\) are constants.

(a) Find \(a,b,c\).

(b) Let \(g(x)=3x^2-7x+2\). Someone claims that \(f(x)=g(x)\) has 3 distinct real roots. Do you agree? Explain.

### 中文詳解

(a) 展開及比較係數得 \(a=6\)、\(b-2a=k\)、\(c-2b=2-k\)、\(-2c=-6\)。故 \(c=3\)、\(b=k+12\)，由 \(3-2(k+12)=2-k\) 得 \(k=-23\)、\(b=-11\)，所以 \(a=6,b=-11,c=3\)。

(b) 相減得 \[\begin{gathered}f-g\\=6x^3-26x^2+32x-8\end{gathered}\]

\(f-g=2(x-2)^2(3x-1)\)。

相異實根只有2及 \(1/3\)，其中2是重根，因此不同意。保留因式2；它不影響零點。

### English Solution

(a) Expansion gives coefficients \(a=6\), \(b-2a=k\), \(c-2b=2-k\), \(-2c=-6\). Hence \(c=3\), \(b=k+12\), and \(3-2(k+12)=2-k\), so \(k=-23\) and \(b=-11\). Thus \(a=6,b=-11,c=3\).

(b) Subtract \(g\) to get \[\begin{gathered}f-g\\=6x^3-26x^2+32x-8\end{gathered}\]

\(f-g=2(x-2)^2(3x-1)\).

The distinct real roots are \(2\) and \(1/3\) only; 2 is repeated. Disagree. The factor2 is retained; it does not change the zero set.

### 中文題目

設 \(p(x)\) 為三次多項式，被 \(x+1\) 及 \(x-2\) 除時的餘式分別為 \(-1\) 及5。

(a) 求它被 \(x^2-x-2\) 除時的餘式。

(b) 已知 \(p(x)\) 被 \(x^2-x-2\) 除時的商式與餘式相同。有人聲稱 \(p(x)=0\) 有兩個有理根。你是否同意？解釋。

### English Question

Let \(p(x)\) be cubic. Its remainders on division by \(x+1\) and \(x-2\) are \(-1\) and 5 respectively.

(a) Find the remainder when divided by \(x^2-x-2\).

(b) The quotient and remainder are the same when \(p(x)\) is divided by \(x^2-x-2\). Someone claims \(p(x)=0\) has two rational roots. Do you agree? Explain.

### 中文詳解

(a) 設餘式為 \(Ax+B\)。代入除式的根得 \(-A+B=-1\)、\(2A+B=5\)，故 \(A=2,B=1\)，餘式為 \(2x+1\)。

(b) 商式亦為 \(2x+1\)，所以 \[\begin{gathered}p(x)=(2x+1)(x^2-x-2)\\+(2x+1)\end{gathered}\]

\(p(x)=(2x+1)(x^2-x-1)\)。

各根為 \(-1/2\) 及 \((1\pm\sqrt5)/2\)，恰有一個有理根，所以不同意「有兩個有理根」。來源解答最後寫「同意」，但與其列出的根矛盾；此處保留原題並修正答案誤植。

### English Solution

(a) Write the remainder as \(Ax+B\). At the divisor roots, \(-A+B=-1\) and \(2A+B=5\). Thus \(A=2,B=1\), giving remainder \(2x+1\).

(b) The quotient is also \(2x+1\). Hence \[\begin{gathered}p(x)=(2x+1)(x^2-x-2)\\+(2x+1)\end{gathered}\]

\(p(x)=(2x+1)(x^2-x-1)\).

The roots are \(-1/2\) and \((1\pm\sqrt5)/2\). Exactly one is rational, so disagree with the claim of two rational roots. The source last sentence says agree, contradicting its own root list; the original question is retained and that answer-key error is corrected.

### 中文題目

三次多項式 \(f(x)\) 可被 \(x+2\) 整除，被 \(x^2-4\) 除時餘式為 \(25x+k\)，其中 \(k\) 為常數。

(a) 求 \(k\)。

(b) 又知 \(3x-1\) 是因式，而被 \(x-1\) 除時餘式為12。有人聲稱 \(f(x)=0\) 有三個相異實數解。聲稱是否正確？解釋。

### English Question

The cubic \(f(x)\) is divisible by \(x+2\). When divided by \(x^2-4\), its remainder is \(25x+k\), where \(k\) is a constant.

(a) Find \(k\).

(b) Also \(3x-1\) is a factor, and division by \(x-1\) gives remainder 12. Someone claims \(f(x)=0\) has three distinct real solutions. Is this correct? Explain.

### 中文詳解

(a) 設 \[\begin{gathered}f(x)=(Ax+B)(x^2-4)\\{}+25x+k\end{gathered}\]。代入 \(x=-2\) 得 \(0=-50+k\)，故 \(k=50\)。

(b) 代入 \(1/3\)，由因式條件得 \(A/3+B=15\)；代入1，由 \(f(1)=12\) 得 \(A+B=21\)，聯立得 \(A=9,B=12\)。

\(f(x)=9x^3+12x^2-11x+2\)。

\(f(x)=(x+2)(3x-1)^2\)。

相異實數解只有 \(-2\) 及 \(1/3\)，後者是重根，故聲稱不正確。

### English Solution

(a) Put \[\begin{gathered}f(x)=(Ax+B)(x^2-4)\\{}+25x+k\end{gathered}\] At \(x=-2\), \(0=-50+k\), so \(k=50\).

(b) At \(x=1/3\), the factor condition gives \(A/3+B=15\). At \(x=1\), \(f(1)=12\) gives \(A+B=21\). Thus \(A=9,B=12\).

\(f(x)=9x^3+12x^2-11x+2\).

\(f(x)=(x+2)(3x-1)^2\).

The distinct real solutions are \(-2\) and \(1/3\); the latter is repeated. The claim is incorrect.
