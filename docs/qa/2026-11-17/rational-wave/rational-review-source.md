### English Question

\[\begin{gathered}\equiv\frac P{x+7}\\+\frac Q{x-8}\end{gathered}\]，求 \(P,Q\)。

B. \(P=3,Q=9\)
C. \(P=4,Q=5\)

### English Question

Let \(P,Q\) be constants. If \(\frac{9x+3}{(x+7)(x-8)}\)

\[\begin{gathered}\equiv\frac P{x+7}\\+\frac Q{x-8}\end{gathered}\] find \(P,Q\).

A. \(P=9,Q=3\)
B. \(P=3,Q=9\)
C. \(P=4,Q=5\)
D. \(P=5,Q=4\)

### 中文詳解

在 \(x\ne-7,8\) 下同乘公分母，得 \[\begin{gathered}9x+3\\=P(x-8)+Q(x+7)\end{gathered}\]。所得多項式恆等式對所有 \(x\) 皆成立。代入 \(-7\) 得 \(-60=-15P\)，故 \(P=4\)；代入 \(8\) 得 \(75=15Q\)，故 \(Q=5\)。檢查：\(4(x-8)+5(x+7)=9x+3\)。

### English Solution

Let \(f(x)=3x^3-19x^2+16x+24\).

(b) Hence factorise \(3x^3-19x^2+16x+20\).

### English Solution

### 中文題目

設 \(f(x)=x^3-ax^2-2x+4b\) 及 \(g(x)=x^3-bx^2-5ax-18\)，其中 \(a,b\) 為常數。

\(f(x)\) 被 \(x+1\) 除時餘式為20，而 \(g(x)\) 可被 \(x+1\) 整除。

(a) 求 \(a,b\)。(b) 因式分解 \(f(x),g(x)\)。

(c) 化簡 \[\begin{gathered}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\\-\frac3{x-9}\end{gathered}\]

### English Question

Let \(f(x)=x^3-ax^2-2x+4b\) and \(g(x)=x^3-bx^2-5ax-18\), where \(a,b\) are constants.

When \(f(x)\) is divided by \(x+1\), the remainder is 20. Also \(g(x)\) is divisible by \(x+1\).

(a) Find \(a,b\). (b) Factorise \(f(x),g(x)\).

(c) Simplify \[\begin{gathered}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\\-\frac3{x-9}\end{gathered}\]

### 中文詳解

(a) 條件給 \(1-a+4b=20\)、\(-19-b+5a=0\)，聯立 \(-a+4b=19\)、\(5a-b=19\) 得 \(a=5,b=6\)。

(b) 代入並分解得 \(f(x)=(x+2)(x-3)(x-4)\)、\(g(x)=(x+1)(x+2)(x-9)\)。

(c) 約去 \(x+2\)，但保留原本排除的值。通分後分子為 \((x-3)(x-4)-3(x+1)\)，即 \[\begin{gathered}x^2-10x+9\\=(x-1)(x-9)\end{gathered}\]。結果為 \((x-1)/(x+1)\)，其中 \(x\ne-1,-2,9\)。

### English Solution

(a) The conditions give \(1-a+4b=20\) and \(-19-b+5a=0\). Solving \(-a+4b=19\), \(5a-b=19\) gives \(a=5,b=6\).

(b) Substitute and factor: \(f(x)=(x+2)(x-3)(x-4)\); \(g(x)=(x+1)(x+2)(x-9)\).

(c) Cancel \(x+2\), retaining its original exclusion. The common numerator is \((x-3)(x-4)-3(x+1)\), which equals \[\begin{gathered}x^2-10x+9\\=(x-1)(x-9)\end{gathered}\] The result is \((x-1)/(x+1)\), with \(x\ne-1,-2,9\).
