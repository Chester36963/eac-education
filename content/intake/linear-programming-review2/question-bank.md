### 中文題目
考慮以下不等式組。\[\begin{cases}3x-4y\le12\\3x+8y\le12\\3x+2y\ge-6\end{cases}\]設其解集區域（包括邊界）為 \(R\)。若 \((x,y)\) 位於 \(R\)，求 \(5x-2y+9\) 的最小值。

### English question
Consider the following system of inequalities. \[\begin{cases}3x-4y\le12\\3x+8y\le12\\3x+2y\ge-6\end{cases}\]Let \(R\) be its solution region (including the boundary). If \((x,y)\) lies in \(R\), find the least value of \(5x-2y+9\).

A. \(-49\) / \(-49\)
B. \(-17\) / \(-17\)
C. \(15\) / \(15\)
D. \(29\) / \(29\)

### 中文解答
三個可行頂點為 \((-4,3),(0,-3),(4,0)\)。代入 \(5x-2y+9\) 得 \(-17,15,29\)，故最小值為 \(-17\)，選 B。

### English solution
The three feasible vertices are \((-4,3),(0,-3),(4,0)\). The values of \(5x-2y+9\) are respectively \(-17,15,29\). Therefore the minimum is \(-17\), option B.

Final answer: B
