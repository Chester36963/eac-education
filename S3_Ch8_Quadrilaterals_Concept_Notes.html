Chapter 8 · Quadrilaterals

8.0Pre-learning: Angles and Parallel Lines

預備知識 · 平行線與角

Every result about quadrilaterals in this chapter rests on three facts about parallel lines. Learn to spot the F, the Z and the U, and the rest of the chapter becomes easy.

AThree angle pairs截線造成的三對角

Concept概念

A transversal（截線） is a straight line that cuts two other lines. When the two lines are parallel（平行） (marked with arrows ›), the transversal creates three special pairs of angles.

Corresponding angles
同位角 · F-shape · a = b
Alternate angles
錯角 · Z-shape · a = b
Interior angles
同旁內角 · U-shape · a + b = 180°
即係話

一條直線「斬」過兩條平行線，就會整出好多個角。我哋唔使逐個記，只要認得三個字母形狀：F、Z、U。

點樣認？

用筆沿住兩個角嘅邊描一次：描到似 F 就係同位角；似 Z 就係錯角；似 U（兩個角喺兩條平行線中間、同一邊）就係同旁內角。

Key points重點記住
  1. F and Z: the two angles are equal.
  2. U: the two angles are supplementary（互補）, i.e. they add up to 180°.
  3. These facts only work when the two lines are parallel. Always look for the arrow marks first.
口訣

「F 同 Z，一樣大；U 字形，加埋一百八。」

BTwo directions: using vs. proving性質 vs 判定：兩個方向

The same angle facts work in two directions. Your reason must show which direction you are going.

Angle pairKnow AB ∥ CD → find anglesKnow angles → prove AB ∥ CD
Corresponding (F)a = b (corr. ∠s, AB ∥ CD)a = b (corr. ∠s equal)
Alternate (Z)a = b (alt. ∠s, AB ∥ CD)a = b (alt. ∠s equal)
Interior, same side (U)a + b = 180° (int. ∠s, AB ∥ CD)a + b = 180° (int. ∠s supp.)
即係話

左邊：已知平行，用嚟計角，理由要寫埋「AB ∥ CD」。
右邊：已知角，用嚟證平行，理由寫「equal」或者「supp.」。

生活例子

好似「落雨 → 地濕」同「地濕 → 證明落過雨？」，方向唔同，講法就唔同。

P1

CTraps to avoid陰濕位

✗To prove AB ∥ CD:
∠a = ∠b (alt. ∠s, AB ∥ CD)
✓To prove AB ∥ CD:
∠a = ∠b, ∴ AB ∥ CD (alt. ∠s equal)
✗The lines look parallel, so x = 70° (corr. ∠s).
✓Check for arrow marks or the words “AB ∥ CD” first. No ∥, no angle fact.
✗Interior angles are equal: x = 110°.
✓Interior angles add up to 180°: x = 180° − 110° = 70°.
點解錯？

第一個：要證平行，你就唔可以用「AB ∥ CD」做理由，咁即係用個答案證個答案，循環論證，扣分。

第二個：「睇落平行」唔等於平行。冇箭咀、題目冇講，就唔准用。
第三個：U 形係加埋 180°，唔係相等，好多人中伏。

8.1Basic Knowledge of Quadrilaterals

四邊形的基本知識

Before studying special quadrilaterals, we need a common language: which sides and angles are “neighbours”, which are “opposite”, and what a diagonal is.

AParts of a quadrilateral四邊形的組成部分

Concept概念

A quadrilateral（四邊形） is a polygon with four sides. We name it by going round its vertices in order, e.g. ABCD.

TermMeaningIn ABCD
adjacent sides（鄰邊）share a common vertexAB, BC
opposite sides（對邊）not adjacentAB, DC
adjacent angles（鄰角）share a common side∠A, ∠B
opposite angles（對角）not adjacent∠A, ∠C
diagonal（對角線）joins two non-adjacent verticesAC, BD
生活化理解

當四個頂點係四間屋：隔籬屋就係「鄰」；對面屋就係「對」；由一間屋斜斜行過對面嗰條路，就係對角線。

Key points重點記住
  1. Every quadrilateral has 2 diagonals, 2 pairs of opposite sides and 2 pairs of opposite angles.
  2. Angle sum of a quadrilateral（四邊形內角和） = (4 − 2) × 180° = 360° (∠ sum of polygon).
  3. Why 360°? One diagonal cuts it into two triangles, and each triangle gives 180°.
✗Quadrilateral ACBD (letters out of order).
✓Name the vertices in order round the figure: ABCD.
✗AB is a diagonal of ABCD.
✓AB is a side. The diagonals are AC and BD.
口訣

「同一點就係鄰，唔鄰就係對，斜斜過對面就係對角線。」

CFive special quadrilaterals五種特殊四邊形

Trapezium（梯形）: a quadrilateral with only one pair of parallel opposite sides.

即係話：得一對對邊平行（唔係兩對）。

Parallelogram（平行四邊形）: two pairs of parallel opposite sides.

即係話：兩對對邊都平行，好似一個被人推歪咗嘅長方形。

Rhombus（菱形）: four equal sides.

即係話：四條邊一樣長，好似一粒「菱角」或者撲克牌嘅階磚。

Rectangle（長方形）: four equal interior angles (each 90°).

即係話：四隻角一樣大，所以每隻都係 360° ÷ 4 = 90°。

Square（正方形）: four equal sides and four equal interior angles.

即係話：菱形同長方形嘅條件一齊有。

家族關係

正方形係「全能」：佢係菱形，亦係長方形，亦係平行四邊形。
但倒轉講就唔得：菱形唔一定係正方形。

記法

睇定義只問兩件事：
① 邊：幾多對平行？幾條一樣長？
② 角：四隻角係咪一樣大？

小心

本課程嘅梯形係「只有一對」平行邊，所以平行四邊形唔係梯形。

標記點睇

箭咀 › = 平行（兩組就用 › 同 »）。
短劃 | = 一樣長。
小正方形 ∟ = 直角 90°。
睇圖第一步：先搵晒呢三種記號。

Traps陰濕位
✗A parallelogram is a special trapezium.
✓A trapezium has only one pair of parallel sides, so a parallelogram is not a trapezium.
✗A rhombus is a square.
✓A square is always a rhombus, but a rhombus need not have right angles.
✗A rectangle has four equal sides.
✓A rectangle has four equal angles. Its sides need not all be equal.
點解錯？

定義講「邊」定「角」要睇清楚：菱形講邊，長方形講角，正方形兩樣都要。
考試問「Is ABCD a square?」，要兩樣條件都證到先得。

8.2Parallelograms

平行四邊形

A parallelogram is defined by its parallel sides, yet it hides three more facts about its sides, angles and diagonals. These three facts are the tools you will use in almost every question of this chapter.

AProperties of parallelograms平行四邊形的性質

Concept概念

A parallelogram（平行四邊形） is a quadrilateral with two pairs of parallel opposite sides. Every parallelogram also has the following three properties（性質）.

(a) Opposite sides are equal
AB = DC, AD = BC
(b) Opposite angles are equal
∠A = ∠C, ∠B = ∠D
(c) Diagonals bisect each other
AE = EC, BE = ED
即係話

平行四邊形「對住嗰對」一定一樣：對邊一樣長，對角一樣大。兩條對角線喺中間交叉，會互相平分（bisect），即係交點係兩條線嘅中點。

小心

「互相平分」唔係「一樣長」！兩條對角線通常唔等長，只係各自被切開一半。

Why is it true?點解會咁？

Join the diagonal BD. By alternate angles (AB ∥ DC and AD ∥ BC), the two triangles share two pairs of equal angles and the common side BD, so △ABD ≅ △CDB (ASA). Matching parts of congruent triangles give the equal sides and equal angles.

Reasons to write理由寫法
Property usedReason code in your solution
opposite sides equal(opp. sides of ∥gram)
opposite angles equal(opp. ∠s of ∥gram)
diagonals bisect each other(diagonals of ∥gram)
adjacent angles add up to 180°(int. ∠s, AB ∥ DC)
生活化理解

用剪刀沿對角線剪開，兩塊三角形可以完全疊埋。疊得埋，對應嘅邊同角自然一樣。

Key points重點記住
  1. Three properties: opposite sides, opposite angles, diagonals bisect.
  2. Two adjacent angles of a parallelogram add up to 180° (they are interior angles).
  3. You may use these properties only after you know the figure is a parallelogram.
口訣

「對邊等、對角等、對角線互相平分；鄰角加埋一百八。」

DWorked examples: combining with Pythagoras例題：配合畢氏定理

Example 3Level 2 PT ∥ QS. R lies on QS such that RP ∥ ST, RT ∥ QP and ∠QPR = 90°. TR = 20 cm and RP = 21 cm. Find (a) PT, (b) QS, (c) the perimeter of PQST.
(a) ∠PRT = ∠QPR = 90°(alt. ∠s, RT ∥ QP)
PT = √20² + 21² cm = 29 cm(Pyth. theorem)
(b) PQRT is a ∥gram, so QR = PT(by definition; opp. sides of ∥gram)
PRST is a ∥gram, so RS = PT(by definition; opp. sides of ∥gram)
QS = 29 + 29 = 58 cm
(c) PQ = TR = 20 cm, ST = RP = 21 cm(opp. sides of ∥gram)
Perimeter = 29 + 20 + 58 + 21 = 128 cm
思路

一個圖入面其實有兩個平行四邊形：PQRT 同 PRST。用兩隻顏色筆分別描出嚟，對邊相等就一目了然。

點樣搵？

見到「∥」嘅條件有兩對，就試吓砌出「兩對對邊都平行」嘅四邊形。PT ∥ QR 加 RT ∥ QP → PQRT；PT ∥ RS 加 RP ∥ ST → PRST。

小心

(b) 要先講「PQRT is a ∥gram (by definition)」，之後先可以用 opp. sides of ∥gram。

Example 4Level 2 ABCD is a parallelogram. AC and BD meet at O. AB = 5 cm, AD = 13 cm and OD = 6 cm. (a) Find ∠ABD. (b) Find AC, correct to 3 significant figures.
(a) OB = OD = 6 cm, so BD = 12 cm(diagonals of ∥gram)
AB² + BD² = 25 + 144 = 169 = AD²
∠ABD = 90°(converse of Pyth. theorem)
(b) OA = √5² + 6² cm = √61 cm(Pyth. theorem)
OC = OA = √61 cm(diagonals of ∥gram)
AC = 2√61 cm = 15.6 cm (3 sig. fig.)
小心

5、12、13 係一組畢氏三元數。見到就要諗：「係咪有直角？」用 converse of Pyth. theorem 證，唔係 Pyth. theorem。

FConditions for parallelograms平行四邊形的判定條件

Concept概念

Section A answered “If it is a parallelogram, what do we know?”. Now we turn it round: “What must we show to prove that a quadrilateral is a parallelogram?”. Besides the definition (two pairs of parallel sides), any one of the following four conditions is enough.

(a) Both pairs of opposite sides equal
(opp. sides equal)
(b) Both pairs of opposite angles equal
(opp. ∠s equal)
(c) Diagonals bisect each other
(diags. bisect each other)
(d) One pair of sides equal and parallel
(2 sides equal and ∥)
即係話

上一部分係「已知係∥gram → 用性質」。
今次係倒轉：「想證佢係∥gram → 要搵咩證據」。

記法

(a)(b)(c) 其實就係三個性質倒轉講。
(d) 係新嘅：同一對邊，要又等長、又平行，兩樣都要。

生活化理解

好似認朋友：佢著咩衫係「性質」；你要搵出「證據」先可以話「係佢」。四個條件，有一個成立就足夠。

Key points重點記住
  1. Properties (Section A) are used after we know it is a parallelogram; conditions (this section) are used to prove it is one.
  2. Condition (d) needs the same pair of sides to be both equal and parallel.
  3. Write the condition as the reason on the last line, e.g. “∴ ABCD is a parallelogram (opp. ∠s equal)”.
口訣

「邊、角、對角線，倒轉講就係判定；一對邊，又等又平行。」

Traps陰濕位
✗AB = DC and AD ∥ BC, so ABCD is a parallelogram.
✓Equal and parallel must be the same pair: AB = DC and AB ∥ DC. (A trapezium can have AB = DC, AD ∥ BC.)
✗KLMN is not yet known to be a ∥gram, but “∠K = ∠M (opp. ∠s of ∥gram)”.
✓You cannot use a property of the figure you are trying to prove. Find the angles another way (e.g. ∠ sum of polygon).
點解錯？

第一個：一對等長、另一對平行，可以係等腰梯形，唔一定係∥gram。
第二個：仲未證到係∥gram，就唔准用∥gram 嘅性質，否則係循環論證。

8.3Rhombuses, Rectangles, Squares and Trapeziums

菱形、長方形、正方形及梯形

A rhombus, a rectangle and a square are all parallelograms, so they inherit every property you have just learnt. Each of them also adds something special of its own.

AProperties of rhombuses菱形的性質

Concept概念

A rhombus（菱形） is a quadrilateral with four equal sides. It has:

  1. all the properties of a parallelogram (opposite sides, opposite angles, diagonals bisect each other);
  2. diagonals that are perpendicular（互相垂直） to each other;
  3. diagonals that bisect the opposite angles（平分對角）.
Diagonals ⊥: ∠AOD = 90°
Each diagonal bisects two angles
即係話

菱形係「加強版」平行四邊形：∥gram 有嘅佢全部有，仲多兩樣：對角線成十字（90°），同埋對角線將每隻角切開一半。

生活化理解

風箏嘅骨架：兩支竹十字交叉，一定係直角。對摺菱形，兩邊會完全重疊，所以對角線將角平分。

理由寫法

兩個新性質都寫 (property of rhombus)。

Example 8Level 1 ABCD is a rhombus. AC and BD meet at O. ∠ADB = 70°. Find m, x (= ∠AOD), y and z.
BC = AB, so m = 5(by definition)
x = ∠AOD = 90°(property of rhombus)
In △AOD: y + 90° + 70° = 180°(∠ sum of △)
y = 20°
z = y = 20°(property of rhombus (AC bisects ∠DAB))
思路

見到菱形 + 對角線：即刻標 90°，再諗「一隻角切一半」。

Example 9Level 2 A kite ABCD is a rhombus. Its diagonals meet at E, AE = 24 cm and BD = 20 cm. Find (a) the perimeter and (b) the area of the kite.
(a) BE = ½ × 20 = 10 cm, ∠AEB = 90°(property of rhombus)
AB = √24² + 10² = 26 cm(Pyth. theorem)
Perimeter = 4 × 26 = 104 cm(by definition)
(b) Area = 2 × area of △ABD = 2 × ½ × 20 × 24
= 480 cm²
小技巧

菱形面積 = ½ × 兩條對角線相乘。
呢度：½ × 20 × 48 = 480 cm²，同答案一樣。

BProperties of rectangles長方形的性質

Concept概念

A rectangle（長方形） is a quadrilateral with four equal interior angles. It has:

  1. all the properties of a parallelogram;
  2. all interior angles are right angles (each 360° ÷ 4 = 90°);
  3. diagonals that are equal in length;
  4. diagonals that cut each other into four equal segments: AE = EB = EC = ED.
即係話

長方形對角線等長又互相平分，所以由中心 E 去四個角一樣遠。

生活化理解

好似手機個 mon：中心點去四隻角距離一樣，所以 △ABE、△BCE … 全部都係等腰三角形。

Example 10Level 1 ABCD is a rectangle. AC and BD meet at E. AB = 4 and BC = 3. Find EB.
∠ABC = 90°(property of rectangle)
AC = √4² + 3² = 5(Pyth. theorem)
BD = AC = 5(property of rectangle)
EB = ½BD = 5/2(property of rectangle)
思路

EB 係半條對角線。先用畢氏定理計 AC，再用「對角線等長、互相平分」。

Example 11Level 2 ABCD is a rectangle and BCED is a parallelogram. ADE, AOC and BOD are straight lines. CE = 10 cm and ∠AOB = 126°. Find (a) AC, (b) ∠AEC.
(a) AC = BD(property of rectangle)
BD = CE = 10 cm, so AC = 10 cm(opp. sides of ∥gram)
(b) OD = OA, so ∠ODA = ∠OAD(property of rectangle; base ∠s, isos. △)
∠ODA + ∠OAD = 126°(ext. ∠ of △)
∠ODA = 63°
∠AEC = ∠ODA = 63°(corr. ∠s, CE ∥ BD)
思路

BD 有兩重身份：佢係長方形嘅對角線，亦係∥gram BCED 嘅一條邊。搵到呢條「橋」，(a) 就解決。

CProperties of squares正方形的性質

Concept概念

A square（正方形） has four equal sides and four equal angles, so it is both a rhombus and a rectangle. It has:

  1. all the properties of a rectangle (right angles, equal diagonals);
  2. all the properties of a rhombus (diagonals ⊥, diagonals bisect the angles);
  3. diagonals that make 45° with the sides.
即係話

正方形係「全能」：長方形、菱形嘅性質佢全部有。90° 嘅角俾對角線平分，就係 45°。

Example 12Level 1 ABCD is a square with diagonals meeting at E, and AC = 2. Find (a) BE, (b) the perimeter of ABCD (leave √ in the answer).
(a) AE = ½AC = 1, BE = AE = 1(property of square)
(b) ∠AEB = 90°(property of square)
AB = √1² + 1² = √2(Pyth. theorem)
Perimeter = 4 × √2 = 4√2(by definition)
小技巧

正方形對角線切出嚟嘅四個三角形，全部係等腰直角三角形（45°–45°–90°）。

Example 13Level 2 ABCD is a square. ∠CBE = 68° and CE = DA. Find x, y and z.
CE = DA = CB(given; by definition)
∠CEB = ∠CBE = 68°(base ∠s, isos. △)
x = 180° − 68° − 68° = 44°(∠ sum of △)
∠DCE = 90° + 44° = 134°(property of square)
CD = CE, so 2y + 134° = 180°, y = 23°(base ∠s, isos. △; ∠ sum of △)
∠CDB = 45°, so z = 45° − 23° = 22°(property of square)
思路

CE = DA，而正方形四邊相等，所以 CE = CB = CD。一個圖入面有兩個等腰三角形：△CBE 同 △CDE。

Quick check即時練習

DTrapeziums梯形

Concept概念

A trapezium（梯形） has only one pair of parallel sides. The parallel sides are the bases（底）; the perpendicular distance between them is the height（高）.

Bases and height
Right-angled trapezium
直角梯形 · a side ⊥ the bases
Isosceles trapezium
等腰梯形 · non-parallel sides equal

In an isosceles trapezium ABCD (AD ∥ BC, AB = DC): base angles（底角） are equal (∠B = ∠C, ∠A = ∠D) and the diagonals are equal (AC = BD).

即係話

等腰梯形「左右對稱」：沿中間一條直線對摺，左右完全重疊。所以底角一樣、對角線一樣長。

小心

等腰梯形對角線等長，但唔係互相平分。同長方形唔同，唔好撈亂。

Example 14Level 2 PQRS is a trapezium, PS ∥ QR and PQ = SR. ∠PQS = 86° and ∠QSR = 26°. Find ∠SQR.
Let ∠SQR = x. PQRS is an isosceles trapezium.(given PQ = SR)
∠SRQ = ∠PQR = x + 86°(base ∠s of isos. trapezium)
x + 26° + (x + 86°) = 180°(∠ sum of △)
2x = 68°, ∠SQR = 34°
The family tree四邊形家族圖
點睇？

箭咀向下 = 「下面嗰個有晒上面嗰個嘅所有性質」。
正方形喺最底：佢係長方形、菱形、平行四邊形。
梯形自己一支，唔係平行四邊形。

✓They are perpendicular and bisect each other. Equal diagonals belong to a rectangle.
✗The diagonals of a rectangle are perpendicular.

8.4Proofs Related to Parallelograms

平行四邊形的相關證明

Now we put everything together. A geometric proof is a chain: each line follows from a given fact, a property, or a line already proved. Learning to build the chain is the goal of this section.

AHow to write a proof點樣寫證明

A four-step routine四步曲
  1. Mark the figure. Copy every given fact onto the figure: ticks, arcs, ∥ arrows, right angles.
  2. Work backwards. Ask: “To prove AF = BE, which two triangles contain them?”
  3. Choose the tool. Congruent triangles (SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, RHS), or a condition for a parallelogram.
  4. Write one fact per line, each with its reason. End with the conclusion and its reason.
即係話

證明好似砌積木：每一粒都要有根據（given、性質、或者上面證過嘅）。唔可以「睇落係」就當係。

口訣

「標圖、倒推、揀工具、逐行寫理由。」

Example 15Level 1 ABCD is a parallelogram and FDEC is a straight line. AF ⊥ FC and BE ⊥ FC. Prove that AF = BE.
In △AFD and △BEC:
∠AFD = ∠BEC = 90°(given)
∠ADF = ∠BCE(corr. ∠s, AD ∥ BC)
AD = BC(opp. sides of ∥gram)
∴ △AFD ≅ △BEC(AAS)
∴ AF = BE(corr. sides, ≅△s)
思路

AF 同 BE 分別喺 △AFD 同 △BEC 入面。兩隻直角 + 一對同位角 + 一對邊 → AAS。

Example 16Level 2 PQRS is a rectangle. AC and BD meet at O. AP = CR and BQ = DS. Prove that AC and BD bisect each other.
Join AB, BC, CD, DA.
PB = PQ − BQ = SR − DS = RD(property of rectangle; given)
△APB ≅ △CRD (AP = CR, ∠P = ∠R = 90°, PB = RD)(SAS)
∴ AB = CD(corr. sides, ≅△s)
Similarly △BQC ≅ △DSA, ∴ BC = DA(SAS; corr. sides, ≅△s)
∴ ABCD is a parallelogram(opp. sides equal)
∴ AC and BD bisect each other(diagonals of ∥gram)
思路

要證「互相平分」，最順手係先證 ABCD 係∥gram，再用「∥gram 對角線互相平分」。判定同性質一齊用。

Example 17Level 2 ABCD is a square. AOCE and BOD are straight lines. (a) Prove that BE = DE. (b) If DE = DB, find ∠EBC.
(a) In △BOE and △DOE:
BO = DO(property of square)
∠BOE = ∠DOE = 90°(property of square)
OE = OE(common side)
∴ △BOE ≅ △DOE, BE = DE(SAS; corr. sides, ≅△s)
(b) DE = DB = BE, so △BDE is equilateral(given; proved in (a))
∠DBE = 60°, ∠DBC = 45°(property of equil. △; property of square)
∠EBC = 60° − 45° = 15°

(b) 用返 (a) 嘅結果：上一部分證到嘅嘢，下一部分可以用，理由寫 (proved in (a))。

Traps陰濕位
✗△AFD ≅ △BEC (AAS), with no lines listing the three pairs.
✓List the three pairs of equal parts, each with a reason, before writing “≅”.
✗Letters out of order: △AFD ≅ △CEB.
✓Match the vertices in order: A↔B, F↔E, D↔C, so △AFD ≅ △BEC.
✗Using AAA or SSA as a congruence reason.

點解錯？

全等符號 ≅ 嘅字母次序要對應，A 對 B、F 對 E…，次序錯咗之後「corr. sides」都會錯。
AAA 只係相似，唔係全等；SSA 唔一定成立。

8.5Mid-point Theorem and Intercept Theorem

中點定理及截線定理

Using what we know about parallelograms, we can now prove two theorems about triangles and parallel lines. They let us find lengths and prove lines parallel without measuring anything.

AMid-point theorem中點定理

Theorem定理

The straight line joining the mid-points（中點） of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and equal to half of it.

In △ABC, if AM = MB and AN = NC, then (a) MN ∥ BC, (b) MN = ½BC. [Reference: (mid-pt. theorem)]

Given: two mid-points
Result: MN ∥ BC, MN = ½BC
Proof idea: produce MN to P with MN = NP
即係話

三角形兩條邊各自搵中點，連埋佢哋：呢條線同第三邊平行，而且長度係佢一半。

生活化理解

好似將三角形「攔腰切一刀」：上面細三角形同原本個形狀一樣，但係邊長全部縮細一半。

點證？

延長 MN 到 P，令 MN = NP。AMCP 對角線互相平分 → ∥gram；再得 MBCP 係 ∥gram（2 sides equal and ∥），所以 MN ∥ BC 同 BC = MP = 2MN。

Example 18Level 1 M and N are the mid-points of DE and DF. EF = 8 cm and ∠DEF = 45°. Find x and MN.
DM = ME and DN = NF(given)
∴ MN ∥ EF(mid-pt. theorem)
x = ∠DEF = 45°(corr. ∠s, MN ∥ EF)
MN = ½EF = ½ × 8 cm = 4 cm(mid-pt. theorem)
Key points重點記住
  1. You need two mid-points on two sides of the same triangle.
  2. The theorem gives two results: the line is parallel, and it is half the third side.
口訣

「兩個中點一條線，平行第三邊，長度得一半。」

P19
Example 19Level 2 D is the mid-point of both AC and BF. E is the mid-point of AB. GCB is a straight line, GC = 4 cm, DE = 2 cm and ∠FGB = 65°. Find (a) CB, (b) ∠ADE.
(a) In △ABC: AD = DC, AE = EB(given)
DE = ½CB, so CB = 2 × 2 = 4 cm(mid-pt. theorem)
(b) In △BFG: BD = DF, BC = CG = 4 cm(given; proved in (a))
∴ DC ∥ FG(mid-pt. theorem)
∠ACB = ∠FGB = 65°(corr. ∠s, DC ∥ FG)
In △ABC: DE ∥ CB(mid-pt. theorem)
∠ADE = ∠ACB = 65°(corr. ∠s, DE ∥ CB)
思路

一個圖有兩個三角形可以用中點定理：△ABC 同 △BFG。(a) 計到 CB = 4 = GC，所以 C 都係中點，(b) 就再用多次。

Example 20Level 2 E, F, G and H are the mid-points of AB, BC, CD and DA. Prove that EFGH is a parallelogram.
Join AC.

∴ EF ∥ HG and EF = HG
∴ EFGH is a parallelogram(2 sides equal and ∥)
有趣結果

BIntercept theorem截線定理

Terms名詞

A transversal（截線） is a line crossing several lines. The length of the segment cut off between two of the lines is an intercept（截距）.

Theorem定理

If three or more parallel lines make equal intercepts on one transversal, they make equal intercepts on any other transversal.

If AB ∥ CD ∥ EF and AC = CE, then BD = DF. [Reference: (intercept theorem)]

即係話

三條平行線，喺第一條斜線上切出一樣長嘅兩段，咁喺第二條斜線上切出嚟嘅兩段都會一樣長。

生活化理解

原子筆紙上面嘅橫間線係平行同等距。用間尺斜斜放上去，每一格喺間尺上量到嘅長度都一樣。

小心

兩條截線上嘅截距唔一定等於對方：AC = CE，BD = DF，但係 AC 唔一定等於 BD。

Special case in a triangle三角形特例

If a line through the mid-point of one side of a triangle is parallel to another side, it bisects the third side.

In △ABC, if AM = MB and MN ∥ BC, then AN = NC. (intercept theorem)

Example 21Level 1 ACEG and BDFH are straight lines with AB ∥ CD ∥ EF ∥ GH. Find x and EG.

思路

第二步用嘅係 AB、EF、GH 三條平行線，截距係 BF 同 FH。揀啱邊三條線好重要。
